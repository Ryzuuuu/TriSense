Every panel drives the real TriSense code: the ModeManager, the Blind Mode engine, the Vosk recogniser and the sign recogniser. Only the hardware is simulated.

Blind Mode · obstacle detection

Drag the obstacles (or use the sliders) to change what each HC-SR04 sensor hears. The real decision engine computes speed, time to collision and proximity, then pulses the motors and speaks.

Scenarios:
Pulse length encodes urgency: 150 ms warning · 300 ms urgent · 500 ms imminent. TTC = approaching fast, proximity = close even if nothing moves.

Deaf Mode · offline captions

Audio goes to the Vosk recogniser running locally, with no Internet. The OLED shows exactly what the 128×64 display on the device would show.
